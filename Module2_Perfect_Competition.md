## 1. Perfectly Competitive Markets / Ринок досконалої конкуренції

### Four Conditions / Чотири умови:

**1. Price taking / Прийняття ціни**
- 🎓 **Official:** Each firm has a negligible market share and cannot influence market price through its decisions.
- 💡 **Simple:** Фірма — «ціноотримувач», бере ціну як дану. Якщо підніме ціну — втратить всіх клієнтів.
- 🧠 **Analogy:** Як продавець на великому базарі — твоя ціна вже встановлена ринком.

**2. Product homogeneity / Однорідність продукту**
- 🎓 **Official:** Products of all firms are perfectly substitutable; no firm can raise price above market value without losing most business.
- 💡 **Simple:** Всі продають однаковий товар — покупцеві все одно, у кого купувати.
- 🧠 **Analogy:** Пшениця є пшениця — ніякої різниці між фермерами.

**3. Free entry and exit / Вільний вхід і вихід**
- 🎓 **Official:** No special entry or exit costs; buyers can easily switch suppliers; sellers can easily enter or exit the market.
- 💡 **Simple:** Немає бар'єрів — будь-хто може зайти на ринок або вийти з нього.

**4. Full information / Повна інформація**
- 🎓 **Official:** All market participants have complete and equal access to information.
- 💡 **Simple:** Всі знають всі ціни та якість товарів.

---

## 2. Demand for a Competitive Firm / Попит для конкурентної фірми

The firm faces a **perfectly elastic (horizontal) demand** at the market price.

```
P = D = AR = MR
```

| Concept | Formula | Meaning (UA) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | P = market price | Ціна = ринкова |
| Demand | D = horizontal line | Попит — горизонтальна лінія |
| Average Revenue | AR = P·q / q = P | Середній дохід = ціна |
| Marginal Revenue | MR = ΔR/Δq = P | Граничний дохід = ціна |

> 🧠 **Key:** If the firm raises its price → loses ALL customers. If it lowers → revenue falls without benefit. So it takes price as given.

---

## 3. Profit Maximization / Максимізація прибутку

### Profit Function / Функція прибутку:
```
π(q) = R(q) − C(q)
```

### First-Order Condition (FOC) / Умова першого порядку:
```
Π' = 0  →  MR(q) = MC(q)
```
Since MR = P under perfect competition:
```
┌─────────────┐
│   P = MC    │  ← The fundamental condition!
└─────────────┘
```

### Second-Order Condition (SOC) / Умова другого порядку:
```
Π'' < 0  →  ∂(MC)/∂q > 0
```
MC must be **increasing** at the optimum (not just any intersection of P and MC).

### Profit on the Graph / Прибуток на графіку:
```
π = (P − AC) · q*  =  area ABCD
```
- If P > AC → **profit** (прибуток)
- If P = AC → **zero profit** (нульовий прибуток)
- If P < AC → **loss** (збиток)

> 💡 **Graphical intuition:** Maximize the **vertical distance** between R(q) = pq (straight line) and C(q) (curve). This happens where the slope of C equals the slope of R, i.e., MC = P.

---

## 4. Shut-Down Rules / Правила зупинки виробництва

| Situation | Decision (SR) | Decision (LR) |
|---|---|---|
| P > AC | ✅ Continue, making profit | ✅ Continue |
| AVC < P < AC | ✅ Continue (covers part of fixed costs) | ❌ Shut down (won't recover full investment) |
| P < AVC | ❌ Shut down immediately | ❌ Shut down |
| P = AVC | Indifferent (shutdown point / точка зупинки) | ❌ Shut down |

> 🧠 **Logic:**
> - **Short run:** Fixed costs are sunk. As long as P ≥ AVC, each unit sold covers variable costs + contributes to fixed costs → better than producing nothing.
> - **Long run:** ALL costs become variable. If P < AC, the firm will not re-invest → exit.

---

## 5. Short-Run Supply Curve / Крива пропозиції у короткостроковому періоді

```
S = MC curve above AVC (the shut-down point)
```

- Below AVC: firm produces q = 0 (shutdown)
- At P = AVC (minimum): firm is indifferent
- Above AVC: firm produces where P = MC

> 📊 **Graphically:** The SR supply curve is the MC curve starting from the minimum of AVC.

---

## 6. Industry Supply / Галузева пропозиція

**Short-run industry supply = Horizontal summation of all individual firm supply curves**

```
Example (3 firms at price P3):
  Firm 1: q₁=5  |  Firm 2: q₂=7  |  Firm 3: q₃=10
  Industry: Q = 5 + 7 + 10 = 22
```

- At lower prices, fewer firms are above their AVC shutdown point → industry supply starts when the first firm's AVC minimum is reached.

---

## 7. Market Equilibrium / Ринкова рівновага

```
QS = QD = Q₀  at price P₀
```

- No excess supply (виробники продали все, що хотіли)
- No excess demand (споживачі купили все, що хотіли)
- Market **clears** → equilibrium

---

## 8. Long-Run Competitive Equilibrium / Довгострокова рівновага

### The Entry/Exit Mechanism / Механізм входу та виходу:

```
π > 0  →  New firms enter  →  S↑  →  P↓  →  π = 0
π < 0  →  Firms exit       →  S↓  →  P↑  →  π = 0
```

**Long-run result:**
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│   P = MC = AC (min AC)  │
└─────────────────────────┘
```
Economic profits = 0 in the long run.

### Types of Industries / Типи галузей (LR supply):

| Industry Type | LR Supply Curve (Sₗ) | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Constant costs | Horizontal (flat) | Entry doesn't change input prices |
| Increasing costs | Upward sloping | Entry raises input prices |
| Decreasing costs | Downward sloping | Entry lowers input prices (scale economies) |

---

## 9. Zero-Profit Condition / Умова нульового прибутку

> ❗ Zero profit does NOT mean the firm earns no money!

- 🎓 **Official:** In long-run equilibrium, competitive firms are compensated for their opportunity costs — there is no superior investment alternative.
- 💡 **Simple:** «Нульовий прибуток» означає нульовий **економічний** прибуток. Бухгалтерський прибуток залишається — він покриває альтернативні витрати (зарплату власника, орендний дохід, тощо).
- 🧠 **Key distinction:** Economic profit = Accounting profit − Opportunity costs

---

## 10. Consumer & Producer Surplus / Надлишок споживача та виробника

### Consumer Surplus (CS) / Надлишок споживача:
- 🎓 **Official:** The sum of additional benefit consumers receive because they pay less than their maximum willingness to pay.
- 💡 **Simple:** Скільки споживачі «зекономили» відносно максимальної ціни, яку готові платити.

```
CS = ½ · (Pmax − P) · Q
```

### Producer Surplus (PS) / Надлишок виробника:
- 🎓 **Official:** Sum over all units produced of the difference between market price and marginal cost of production.
- 💡 **Simple:** Скільки виробники «заробили зверху» відносно мінімальної ціни, за яку погоджуються виробляти.

```
PS = ½ · (P − Pmin) · Q
```

### Important Distinction / Важлива різниця:
```
PS ≠ π  (producer surplus ≠ profit!)
π = PS − C_fix
```
> PS includes the contribution to fixed costs. Profit is what remains after ALL costs.

### Triangle Formula / Формула трикутника:
```
Area = ½ · base · height = ½ · a · b
```

---

## 11. Welfare Efficiency / Ефективність добробуту

### Social Welfare = CS + PS (at market equilibrium = maximum possible)

- **Economic efficiency / Економічна ефективність:** Maximizes aggregated CS + PS → maximizes total social welfare.
- **Pareto efficiency / Парето-ефективність:** Impossible to reallocate to make anyone better off without making someone else worse off.

> ⚠️ Economic efficiency says nothing about **income distribution** (розподіл доходів).

**Key rule:** Any market failure or government intervention creates efficiency loss → DWL.

---

## 12. Market Failure / Провали ринку

🎓 **Official:** Situation where an unregulated competitive market is inefficient because prices do not send correct signals to consumers and producers.

**Four causes / Чотири причини:**

| Type | Description (UA) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Externalities** | Costs/benefits affect third parties not reflected in prices | Pollution (забруднення) |
| **Information failure** | Asymmetric information → one party knows more | Used car market |
| **Market power** | One party can influence market price | Monopoly |
| **Public goods** | Non-rivalrous + non-excludable in consumption | National defense |

---

## 13. Government Intervention / Державне втручання

### Objectives / Цілі:
1. **Redistribution** (перерозподіл): Max price, min price, price support, production quotas, tariffs, import quotas
2. **Steering effects** (регулюючий ефект): Taxes, subsidies

### Deadweight Loss (DWL) / Безповоротні втрати:
- 🎓 **Official:** Inefficient reduction in total economic welfare (net loss of total surplus).
- 💡 **Simple:** Частина загального добробуту, яка зникає назавжди — ні споживачам, ні виробникам, ні державі.
- Arises from: governmental intervention OR market failure.

---

## 14. Welfare Effects Summary Table / Зведена таблиця ефектів добробуту

### 14.1 Price Ceiling / Максимальна ціна (price ceiling, P^max < P₀)

**Effect:** Price forced below equilibrium → quantity falls to Q₁ < Q₀

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **+A − B** (gain from lower price, lose triangle B) |
| ΔPS | **−A − C** (lose area A to consumers, lose C to DWL) |
| ΔWF (DWL) | **−B − C** |

> ⚠️ **Inelastic demand special case:** If demand is very inelastic, area B > A → **consumers may be WORSE off** with the price ceiling!

---

### 14.2 Price Floor / Minimum Price / Мінімальна ціна (price floor, P^min > P₀)

**Effect:** Price forced above equilibrium → quantity falls to Q₁ < Q₀; surplus supply (unemployment at minimum wage!)

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **−A − C** |
| ΔPS | **+A − B − (light blue area)** |
| ΔWF | **−B − C − (light blue area)** |

> 💡 Application: **Minimum wage** = price floor in labor market → creates unemployment (кількість пропозиції праці > попит на працю).

---

### 14.3 Price Support / Цінова підтримка (P_S > P₀, government buys surplus q_G)

**Effect:** Government sets price above equilibrium AND purchases excess supply.

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **−A − B** |
| ΔPS | **+A + B + D** |
| COST GOV | **−(light blue area)** = −P_S · q_G |
| ΔWF | **+D − (light blue area)** |

---

### 14.4 Production Quotas / Виробничі квоти (limit supply to Q₁ < Q₀)

**Effect:** Supply artificially restricted → price rises to P_S.

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **−A − B** |
| ΔPS | **+A + B + D** (without gov payment) |
| COST GOV | **−B − C − D** (payment needed for voluntary restriction) |
| ΔWF | **−B − C** |

---

### 14.5 Import Quota / Ban / Заборона імпорту (open economy)

**Setup:** Free market domestic price = world price P_W < P₀. Ban raises price to P₀.

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **−A − B − C** |
| ΔPS | **+A** |
| ΔWF | **−B − C** |

> Import quota (partial): same structure but the government or quota holders may collect rent on the restricted imports.

---

### 14.6 Tariff / Мито (levy on imported goods, raises price from P_W to P*)

| | Change |
|---|---|
| GOV REV (tariff) | **+D** |
| ΔCS | **−A − B − C − D** |
| ΔPS | **+A** |
| ΔWF | **−B − C** |

> 💡 Tariff vs Import ban: Both create DWL of B+C. Tariff generates government revenue D; ban does not (or quota rent goes to license holders).

---

### 14.7 Taxes / Податки

#### Lump-sum tax on profits (t₁) / Паушальний податок на прибуток:
```
Πnet = (1 − t₁)(p·q − C(q))
FOC: (1 − t₁)(p − MC(q)) = 0  →  P = MC(q)
```
> ✅ **No allocative effect!** Optimal quantity does NOT change. Only profits are reduced.

#### Value Added Tax / consumption tax (t₂) / ПДВ:
```
Πnet = (p − t₂)·q − C(q)
FOC: (p − t₂) − MC(q) = 0  →  P − t₂ = MC(q)
```
> ❌ **Has allocative effect!** Effective price received by seller falls → quantity decreases.

**⚡ Key Rule: Only taxes on consumption (VAT) have allocative effects!**

#### VAT Welfare Effects (slide 34):

**Setup:** Tax t creates a wedge — buyers pay P_b, sellers receive P_s = P_b − t

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **−A − C** |
| ΔPS | **−D − B** |
| GOV REV | **T · q₁ = A + D** |
| ΔWF | **−B − C** |

**Four conditions that must hold after tax is in place:**
1. P_b − P_s = Tax
2. Q^D = Q^S
3. Q^D = Q^D(P_b) — buyers on demand curve at buyer's price
4. Q^S = Q^S(P_s) — sellers on supply curve at seller's price

---

### 14.8 Tax Incidence / Хто реально платить податок

> **Key insight:** The legal burden (хто перераховує) ≠ economic burden (хто реально платить).
> Tax incidence depends on **relative elasticities of supply and demand**.

| Elasticity Condition | Who bears the burden |
|---|---|
| Demand relatively inelastic | **Consumers** pay more |
| Supply relatively inelastic | **Sellers** pay more |
| Perfectly elastic demand | 100% on sellers |
| Perfectly elastic supply | 100% on consumers |

**Formulas:**
```
Tax burden on consumers (%) = εP^QS / (εP^QS − εP^QD)

Tax burden on sellers (%) = −εP^QD / (εP^QS − εP^QD)
```
> Note: εP^QD < 0 (demand elasticity is negative), εP^QS > 0 (supply elasticity is positive).

---

### 14.9 Subsidy / Субсидія

**Effect:** Government pays s per unit → sellers receive P_s > P₀, buyers pay P_b < P₀, quantity rises to Q₁ > Q₀.

**Four conditions after subsidy:**
1. P_S − P_D = Subsidy
2. Q^D = Q^S
3. Q^D = Q^D(P_D) — buyers on demand curve at buyer's price
4. Q^S = Q^S(P_S) — sellers on supply curve at seller's price

| | Change |
|---|---|
| ΔCS | **+A + B** |
| ΔPS | **+D + C** |
| COST GOV | **−S·q₁ = −(A+B+C+D+E)** |
| ΔWF | **−E** (DWL = triangle E) |

> 🧠 **Subsidy always creates DWL** — government pays more than the total gain to consumers and producers!

---

## 15. Worked Example: Subsidy / Розв'язаний приклад

**Given:**
```
Demand:  P = 60 − 3Q
Supply:  P = 3Q
Subsidy: s = 6€ per unit
```

**Step 1: Find baseline equilibrium (без субсидії)**
```
60 − 3Q = 3Q
60 = 6Q  →  Q₀ = 10,  P₀ = 30
```

**Step 2: Apply the 4 conditions after subsidy**
```
Condition 1:  Ps − Pd = 6
Condition 2:  QD = QS  → call it Q*
Condition 3:  Pd = 60 − 3Q*   (buyers on demand curve)
Condition 4:  Ps = 3Q*         (sellers on supply curve)
```

**Step 3: Solve**
```
From cond. 1: Ps = Pd + 6
From cond. 4: 3Q* = Pd + 6
From cond. 3: Pd = 60 − 3Q*

Substitute:   3Q* = (60 − 3Q*) + 6
              3Q* = 66 − 3Q*
              6Q* = 66
              Q* = 11

Ps = 3 · 11 = 33  (price sellers receive)
Pd = 33 − 6 = 27  (price buyers pay)
```

**Step 4: Welfare calculations**
```
ΔCS = area gained by consumers (triangle + rectangle above old P₀, below Pd)
    = ½ · (P₀ − Pd) · (Q* + Q₀)  [trapezoid]
    Wait — use the simpler approach:

Government cost = s · Q* = 6 · 11 = 66

CS gain: buyers pay 27 instead of 30, quantity rises from 10 to 11
ΔCS = ½ · (30 − 27) · (10 + 11) = ½ · 3 · 21 = 31.5  ← (A+B area)

PS gain: sellers receive 33 instead of 30, quantity rises from 10 to 11
ΔPS = ½ · (33 − 30) · (10 + 11) = ½ · 3 · 21 = 31.5  ← (C+D area)

Total gain (CS+PS) = 31.5 + 31.5 = 63
Gov cost           = 66
DWL = Gov cost − (ΔCS + ΔPS) = 66 − 63 = 3  ← triangle E
```

**Answer:**
- New equilibrium quantity: Q* = 11
- Buyers pay: P_D = 27
- Sellers receive: P_S = 33
- Government cost: 66
- DWL: 3

## 16. Exam Focus / На що звернути увагу на іспиті

### 🔑 Most Tested Concepts / Найбільш важливі концепції:

**Profit maximization:**
- Always set P = MC to find q*
- Check SOC: MC must be increasing at optimum
- Calculate π = (P − AC) · q*

**Shutdown:**
- SR: compare P to AVC minimum
- LR: compare P to AC minimum
- Zero-profit ≠ zero earnings (opportunity costs covered)

**CS/PS calculation:**
- Use triangle formula A = ½ · base · height
- PS ≠ profit (PS includes fixed cost contribution)

**Government interventions — standard procedure:**
1. Find baseline Q₀, P₀ (set supply = demand)
2. Apply intervention → find new Q₁, new prices
3. Calculate ΔCS, ΔPS, GOV revenue/cost
4. ΔWF = ΔCS + ΔPS + ΔGov (if applicable)
5. DWL = −ΔWF

**Tax vs Subsidy — 4-condition system:**
```
TAX:     Pb − Ps = t  ;  QD = QS  ;  QD = QD(Pb)  ;  QS = QS(Ps)
SUBSIDY: Ps − Pd = s  ;  QD = QS  ;  QD = QD(Pd)  ;  QS = QS(Ps)
```

**Tax incidence:**
- Who legally pays ≠ who economically bears the burden
- Inelastic side bears more of the burden
- Formula: consumer share = εS/(εS − εD)

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes / Типові помилки:

| Mistake | Correct Approach |
|---|---|
| Confusing PS and π | PS = π + C_fix; PS ≥ π |
| Forgetting DWL under subsidy | Subsidy always has DWL = E (overproduction) |
| Wrong shutdown rule (SR vs LR) | SR: AVC threshold; LR: AC threshold |
| Ignoring SOC for profit max | Check MC is increasing at P=MC |
| Assuming lump-sum tax changes quantity | Lump-sum tax on profits → P=MC unchanged |
| Price ceiling always helps consumers | With inelastic demand, ceiling can HURT consumers (B > A) |
| Forgetting government cost in welfare calc | For subsidies/price support: ΔWF includes −gov cost |

### 📊 Quick Reference: Welfare Effects / Швидка шпаргалка:

| Intervention | ΔCS | ΔPS | ΔGov | ΔWF (DWL) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price ceiling (P^max) | +A−B | −A−C | 0 | −B−C |
| Price floor (P^min) | −A−C | +A−B−(extra) | 0 | −B−C−(extra) |
| Price support | −A−B | +A+B+D | −(Gov cost) | +D−(Gov cost) |
| Production quota | −A−B | +A+B+D | −B−C−D | −B−C |
| Import ban | −A−B−C | +A | 0 | −B−C |
| Tariff | −A−B−C−D | +A | +D | −B−C |
| VAT (cons. tax) | −A−C | −D−B | +(A+D) | −B−C |
| Subsidy | +A+B | +D+C | −(A+B+C+D+E) | −E |

> 💡 **Pattern:** DWL almost always = triangles on either side of original equilibrium quantity. Any reduction or increase in quantity from Q₀ creates a DWL.
